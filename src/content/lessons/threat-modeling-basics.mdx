Threat modeling is a repeatable way to identify what can go wrong.

<Callout type="tip">
Start simple: list your **assets**, **entry points**, and **trust boundaries**.
</Callout>

## A simple loop

1. Map the system
2. Identify threats
3. Prioritize risks
4. Add mitigations

<CodeBlock language="text">
Asset: Customer data
Threat: Unauthorized access
Mitigation: MFA + audit logging
</CodeBlock>
