Welcome to the foundations track. We'll build a mental model of how to think about protecting systems.

<Callout type="info">
Security is about reducing risk, not eliminating it. Focus on measurable improvements.
</Callout>

## The CIA Triad

- **Confidentiality**: prevent unauthorized access
- **Integrity**: prevent unauthorized modification
- **Availability**: keep systems reliable and accessible

# Example: check file integrity with sha256
