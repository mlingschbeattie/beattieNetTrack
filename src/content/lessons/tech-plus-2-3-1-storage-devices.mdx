## Storage Devices

Storage devices hold data persistently — data survives a power cycle. Choosing the right type depends on speed requirements, capacity needs, and budget.

### Hard Disk Drive (HDD)

- **Mechanism:** Spinning magnetic platters read/written by an actuator arm
- **Interface:** SATA (3 Gbps or 6 Gbps)
- **Speed:** 80–160 MB/s sequential read; high latency (5–10ms) due to seek time
- **Capacity:** 1 TB – 20+ TB consumer drives
- **Best for:** Bulk storage, backups, archival — where speed matters less than cost per GB
- **Failure modes:** Mechanical failure, head crash, platter damage

### Solid State Drive (SSD)

- **Mechanism:** NAND flash memory cells (no moving parts)
- **Interface:** SATA or NVMe (PCIe)
- **Speed:** SATA SSD ~550 MB/s; NVMe SSD 3,500–7,000 MB/s
- **Capacity:** 256 GB – 4+ TB consumer SSDs
- **Best for:** OS drive, applications, anything speed-critical
- **Failure modes:** Flash wear (write cycles), controller failure

### NVMe vs. SATA SSD

| | SATA SSD | NVMe SSD |
|---|---|---|
| Interface | SATA bus | PCIe (M.2 or U.2) |
| Max speed | ~550 MB/s | 3,500–7,000 MB/s |
| Latency | ~0.1ms | ~0.02ms |
| Form factor | 2.5" or M.2 SATA | M.2, U.2 |

### Flash Storage

- **USB Flash Drive** — portable, removable; USB 2.0 (slow) or USB 3.x (fast)
- **SD / microSD card** — common in cameras, Raspberry Pi, Android devices; rated by class/speed
- **eMMC** — embedded flash soldered to the board; common in tablets and Chromebooks; slower than NVMe

### Optical Drives

- **CD, DVD, Blu-ray** — spinning optical disc read by a laser
- **Largely legacy** — rare in modern laptops; external USB drives still sold
- **Use cases:** Software distribution (legacy), archival, physical media playback

### Key Exam Points

- HDDs fail mechanically; SSDs fail from write cycle exhaustion
- NVMe is faster than SATA SSD because PCIe has much higher bandwidth than SATA
- **Form factors:** 3.5" (desktop HDD), 2.5" (laptop HDD/SSD), M.2 (NVMe/SATA SSD)
- **IOPS** (Input/Output Operations Per Second) matters more than sequential speed for OS/app workloads
