<Section title="Driver installation lab" subtitle="Hands-on: diagnose and resolve driver issues">
  <p>
    This lab simulates real-world driver failures. Your job is to identify the affected device,
    source the correct driver, and verify stability after installation.
  </p>
  <Callout type="warn">
    Always create a restore point before rolling back or installing new drivers on production systems.
  </Callout>
</Section>

<Section title="Common symptoms">
  <div class="card-grid">
    <Card title="Unknown device">
      <p>Missing driver shows as an unknown device in Device Manager.</p>
    </Card>
    <Card title="Blue screen">
      <p>Faulty GPU or storage drivers can trigger stop errors under load.</p>
    </Card>
    <Card title="Peripheral not detected">
      <p>Outdated chipset or USB drivers prevent devices from enumerating.</p>
    </Card>
  </div>
</Section>

<Section title="Workflow">
  <ol>
    <li>Identify the failing device and capture the hardware ID.</li>
    <li>Download the latest OEM driver package.</li>
    <li>Install, reboot, and verify the device status.</li>
  </ol>
  <CodeBlock language="text">Win + R → devmgmt.msc
Right-click device → Properties → Details → Hardware Ids</CodeBlock>
</Section>
