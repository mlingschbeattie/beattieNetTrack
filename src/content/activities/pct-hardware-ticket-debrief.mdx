Use this guided debrief right after the lesson and before the lab.

## Scenario

A classroom desktop powers on, spins all fans, but never shows video on the monitor.
A junior tech already reseated RAM and replaced thermal paste before checking the monitor input.

## Your Job

For each checkpoint, state the best next action and why:

1. External checks: What should be verified before opening the case?
2. Isolation check: What single swap/test gives the highest signal next?
3. Root-cause claim: What evidence is enough to justify replacing hardware?

## Quality Bar

A strong response is:

- Sequenced in low-risk to high-risk order
- Evidence-based, not assumption-based
- Clear enough that another technician can repeat it

## Demo Prompt

When presenting live, have learners explain their step order out loud before pressing Check/Submit in the workspace.
