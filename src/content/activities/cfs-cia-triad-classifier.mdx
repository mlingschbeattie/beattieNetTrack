### Mission Briefing: SOC Tier-1 Triage

Welcome to the Security Operations Center (SOC). As a Tier-1 Incident Responder, your first responsibility during an active security alert is to classify which property of the **CIA Triad** failed first:

- **🔒 Confidentiality**: Unauthorized disclosure or exposure of private data.
- **🛡️ Integrity**: Unauthorized alteration, tampering, or corruption of data or system logic.
- **⚡ Availability**: Disruption, downtime, or denial of access to authorized users.

### Triage Protocol

For each incident case in your workspace console:

1. **Classify Primary Impact**: Major breaches often have downstream effects, but determine which property broke **first**.
2. **Identify the Threat Vector**: Pinpoint the root cause or attack path that enabled the compromise.
3. **Select Containment Action**: Apply the immediate defensive control to halt active damage before starting forensic investigation.

Select your findings in the interactive workspace console on the right, verify each incident, and submit your final triage report.
