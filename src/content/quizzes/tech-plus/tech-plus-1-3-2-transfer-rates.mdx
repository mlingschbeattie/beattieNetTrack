    prompt: "An internet service provider advertises a downstream connection speed of 100 Mbps. What is the equivalent maximum download speed expressed in megabytes per second (MBps)?"

      - "B. 800 MBps"
      - "C. 12.5 MBps"
      - "D. 50 MBps"

    explanation: "Because there are 8 bits in one byte, divide the bit rate by 8 to calculate the byte rate: 100 Mbps / 8 = 12.5 MBps."

      - "D. 1 Gbps"

      - "C. Latency"
      - "D. Baseband"
    correctIndex: 2
    explanation: "Latency (often measured in milliseconds via ping ICMP echo requests) is the time delay experienced as data packets travel from source to destination and back."
