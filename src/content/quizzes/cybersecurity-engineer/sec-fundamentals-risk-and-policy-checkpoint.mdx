    prompt: "Why does threat modeling begin with identifying assets?"
    options:
      - "Assets are easier to enumerate than attack techniques"
      - "Without knowing what causes harm if lost, you cannot judge which threats matter"
      - "Regulations mandate an asset inventory first"
      - "Attack techniques change too often to be useful"
    correctIndex: 1
    explanation: "An asset is anything whose loss, exposure, or corruption causes harm. Assets give threats their weight, which is what makes prioritization possible."
  - id: q2
    type: single
    prompt: "A web application takes input from the internet and queries an internal database. Where is the critical trust boundary?"
    options:
      - "Between the database and its backups"
      - "Between the untrusted internet and the application forwarding data toward trusted storage"
      - "Between two employees on the same LAN"
      - "Between the office Wi-Fi and the printer"
    correctIndex: 1
    explanation: "A trust boundary is the line between zones of differing trust. Most attacks occur where untrusted input crosses into a more trusted system."
  - id: q3

      - "Repudiation"
      - "Denial of service"
    correctIndex: 0
    explanation: "Spoofing is pretending to be someone you are not, and it maps to the authentication control family."
  - id: q4

      - "Information disclosure"
      - "Elevation of privilege"
      - "Repudiation"
      - "Spoofing"
    correctIndex: 2
    explanation: "Repudiation is performing an action and denying it. The control is auditing — append-only logs with reliable timestamps."
  - id: q5

      - "Denial of service"
      - "Spoofing"
    correctIndex: 0
    explanation: "Information disclosure exposes data to people who should not see it, and it maps to confidentiality."
  - id: q6

      - "Repudiation"
      - "Denial of service"
    correctIndex: 1
    explanation: "Elevation of privilege is gaining access beyond what was authorized, and it maps to the authorization control family. Least privilege is the standard mitigation."
  - id: q7

    correctIndex: 2

    options:

      - "A post-breach incident response plan"
    correctIndex: 1
    explanation: "The value is making threats visible and ranked so they can be tracked and fixed. It is not a guarantee of safety or a complete vulnerability inventory."
