You will be asked for exact numbers here, and exact numbers are the point — this is the one place on the Tech+ exam where you either can do the arithmetic or you cannot. Take the conversions slowly. Nobody does these quickly at first.

## Goal

Convert in both directions, apply the storage-unit prefixes correctly, and explain the advertised-versus-actual capacity gap that every technician gets asked about.
