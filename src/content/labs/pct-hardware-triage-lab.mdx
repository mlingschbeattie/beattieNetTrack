This lab simulates a real bench ticket and grades your troubleshooting process, not guesswork.

## Lab Goal

Demonstrate that you can move from symptom to verified resolution using a repeatable triage sequence.

## Completion Standard

Pass every step and submit a closure note that proves the issue was verified and resolved.
