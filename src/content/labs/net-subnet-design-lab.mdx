The school has been given 192.168.10.0/24 and four areas that must not share a broadcast domain: a 60-seat computer suite, a 25-machine staff room, a 10-device office, and a point-to-point link to the annexe.

## Goal

Size each subnet to its requirement, pick the right masks, and prove you can identify a valid host address from an invalid one.
